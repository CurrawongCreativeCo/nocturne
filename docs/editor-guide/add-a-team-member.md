# Add a team member

<!-- Video: add a link for "add-a-team-member" under help.videos in site.profile.ts. -->

## Steps

1. Open **Team** in the left-hand menu, then click **Create new**.
2. Fill in their **name** and **role**.
3. Upload their **portrait** and fill in its alternative text. Use the same shape for everyone so the cards line up. See "Replace a photo".
4. Add a **short bio**. Two or three sentences is plenty.
5. Click **Publish**.

## Change the order

Drag people up or down in the Team list. The site follows the same order.

## Remove someone

Open their entry, open the menu next to **Publish** and choose **Unpublish** to hide them, or **Delete** to remove them for good.
