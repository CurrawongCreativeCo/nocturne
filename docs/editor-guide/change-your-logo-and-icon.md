# Change your logo or browser icon

The logo and site name show in the header. The browser icon is the small picture in browser tabs and bookmarks.

<!-- Video: add a link for "change-your-logo-and-icon" under help.videos in site.profile.ts. -->

## Steps

1. In the sidebar, open **Website settings**, then **Name, logo & icon**.
2. To change the logo, upload a new one in the **Logo** field. An SVG or a transparent PNG works best.
3. To change the browser icon, find **Browser icon**. Choose **Upload an icon** and add a square picture, or choose **Use the site logo** and crop it square.
4. Check the preview below the icon. At the smallest size, a plain mark reads better than small text.
5. Click **Publish**.

## Good to know

- The browser icon works best as a square PNG or SVG, at least 512 px wide.
- Browsers keep old icons for a while. If you still see the old one, close the tab and reopen the site.
- The site name and description on this screen are also what search engines show.
