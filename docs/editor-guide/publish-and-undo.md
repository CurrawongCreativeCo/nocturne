# Publish and undo

Nothing you edit goes live until you publish it.

<!-- Video: add a link for "publish-and-undo" under help.videos in site.profile.ts. -->

## Publish

1. Make your changes. They save automatically as a **draft**.
2. Read them over. Use **Edit your website** to see the page as visitors will.
3. Click **Publish**. Your site updates within a minute.

## Undo a mistake

- **Not published yet?** Open the menu next to **Publish** and choose **Discard changes**. The page goes back to the live version.
- **Already published?** Open the menu next to **Publish** and choose **Review changes** or **History**. Pick an earlier version and click **Restore**, then publish again.

## Take something off the site

Open the menu next to **Publish** and choose **Unpublish**. The page disappears from the site but stays in the Studio, so you can bring it back later.

## Good to know

- Every version is kept, so you can always go back.
- If two people edit at once, you will see each other's changes appear.
