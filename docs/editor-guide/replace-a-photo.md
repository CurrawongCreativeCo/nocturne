# Replace a photo

A new photo takes the same place as the old one, so the layout stays tidy.

<!-- Video: add a link for "replace-a-photo" under help.videos in site.profile.ts. -->

## Steps

1. Open the page, then open the section that holds the photo.
2. Click the photo, then **Remove** (or drag the new file onto it).
3. Upload your new photo from your computer.
4. Drag the small circle (the **hotspot**) onto the most important part, such as a face. The photo then crops around it.
5. Fill in the **Alternative text** field. See below.
6. Click **Publish**.

## Choosing a good photo

- Each photo field shows a **Recommended** size. Aim for that size or larger.
- Use the right shape for the spot (landscape, portrait or square).
- Avoid photos with words in them. Screen readers cannot read the words.

## Alternative text

Describe what the photo shows in one short sentence, for example "Two electricians fitting a switchboard". It helps people using screen readers and it helps Google. Leave it empty only if the photo is purely decoration.
