# Change a heading

Headings are the large titles on your pages. You can change any of them in a minute.

<!-- Video: add a link for "change-a-heading" under help.videos in site.profile.ts. -->

## Steps

1. Open **Edit your website** from Start here.
2. Click the heading you want to change. It becomes editable.
3. Type the new words. Keep it short: about **8 words** reads best.
4. Click **Publish** when you are happy.

## Good to know

- Each page has **one main heading** at the very top. Other headings in the page are section titles.
- Changes stay as a **draft** until you publish, so nobody sees half-finished work.
- Prefer a form? Open the page in **Pages**, find the section and edit its **Title** field.
