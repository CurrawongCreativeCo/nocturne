**The keep-alive experiment** (`keepalive-race-2026-11-16/`, added by #223; **not a CI log or capture**). Two local runs of a
Node-only, two-process harness on 16 November 2026 (UTC) with Node v24.22.2 (bundled undici 7.30.1) on macOS 26.6.2 arm64: the
first at 07:40:37 (`control`, `race`), the second at 08:02:33 (`default-get`, `candidate-get`, `candidate-post`), each case once,
with no retry or timing search. Node came from `https://nodejs.org/dist/v24.22.2/node-v24.22.2-darwin-arm64.tar.gz` (SHA-256
`aa9549349e47dffaa42dcf95d6e06ade8c4bdafc980979883b88767670df385a`, checked against the published `SHASUMS256.txt`; its GPG
signature was not). Files are byte for byte what ran and what they recorded, so they have the hashes above; its nested
`MANIFEST.md` (metadata, not hashed here) holds the commands, the per-case outcomes and what the experiment does not show. It
shows that a request on a reused connection is lost when the server's keep-alive timer fires before it is read, and that
`keepAliveTimeout: 0` avoids that in the harness. It does not attribute any CI failure to that mechanism, and it was not run
on Linux.
